Privacy Policy — One Left

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

One Left is a puzzle game for Android. Playing it happens offline: one tap sets off a chain reaction, exactly one dot is left — and nothing about that is sent to me or to anyone else. Exactly four things go online, and all four are described separately below: the consent request, the ads themselves (the ads between levels, an ad banner at the bottom edge of the screen and the optional reward videos), purchases through Google Play, and statistics about how the game is used. This policy tells you what the app stores on your device, what is processed on those paths, who receives it, on what legal basis, and what rights you have.

App: One Left (package name com.northloopgames.oneleft), distributed through Google Play.

2. What the app stores on your device

Everything below stays on your device only. There is no account and no server of mine.

  • progress and stars per level
  • best results and the taps needed for the replay
  • daily board history
  • counters and timestamps: attempts, near misses, retries, time anchors
  • settings: sound, vibration, "Send statistics" (field statistik)
  • the purchase state of "Remove Ads"
  • the ad counters: levelSeitInterstitial, interstitialsHeute, interstitialDatum, interstitialsGesamt, letzterWerbungZeitanker
  • the consent answer stored by the UMP SDK

Locations: the files oneleft-save.json and oneleft-save.bak.json in the app's private folder, plus the private settings of the Google SDKs.

Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG for the saved game (storing it is strictly necessary for the game to do what it is for); § 25(1) TDDDG together with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR for everything the ad SDK stores.

Retention: as long as the app is installed. Android's Auto Backup copies these files into the user's Google account; that is a function of the operating system, not of mine. Uninstalling deletes the local data.

4. In-app purchases

There is exactly one product: "Remove Ads", EUR 1.99, non-consumable, handled through Google Play Billing, built into the app through the Unity package com.unity.purchasing 5.4.2 and the Google Play Billing Library.

The app never sees any payment data — no card number, no billing address, no name. From Google Play the app only receives: the product identifier, the order or purchase token, the purchase status, and the time.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the purchase contract).

Because the product is non-consumable, it is restorable: an earlier purchase comes back through "Restore purchases" or by itself on the next start.

State of this version: the purchase path is currently not part of the shipped package; the permission com.android.vending.BILLING is absent from the manifest, and there is no purchase offer in the game. This section applies as soon as the purchase path ships.

5. Ads

5.1 The four ad placements, one by one

  • Ads between levels (interstitial): from level 6 at the earliest, at most every 4th level, at most 8 per day, each with 3 seconds of advance warning. Never in the middle of a level.
  • Ad banner in its own strip at the bottom edge of the screen: from level 6 at the earliest, only on the island map, on the result card and while a chain reaction is running. Never while you are meant to tap the field, never in the daily board, never over the playing field. Text and image ads only, no videos.
  • Reward video "Second spark": optional, only if you tap it.
  • Reward video "Aim help": optional, only if you tap it.

5.2 The SDK

Ads are delivered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), built in as com.google.ads.mobile 11.5.0. The consent request is presented by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), which is part of the same package.

5.3 What Google processes when delivering an ad

  • your device's advertising ID (Werbe-ID)
  • your IP address (truncated or not, according to Google's own statements)
  • device and app details (model, operating system version, language, app version)
  • an approximate location that Google estimates from the IP address — this is not a location permission; the game never reads your location
  • your interaction with the ad (seen, tapped, dismissed)

5.4 Consent first

Without your consent MobileAds.Initialize is never called, no request goes out, and the game is fully playable. Saying no locks nothing.

5.5 Withdrawal

You can withdraw at any time through Settings → Privacy → "Change consent" (Art. 7(3) sentence 4 GDPR: withdrawing must be as easy as giving). Ads already loaded are then discarded, and nothing new is requested.

5.6 Profiling

Whether Google delivers personalised ads depends solely on your consent. If you decline, no ad is requested at all.

5.7 Maximum ad content rating

The maximum ad content rating is set to G — suitable for all ages.

5.8 Transfer to the USA

Google LLC is based in the USA. The transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission issued an adequacy decision on 10 July 2023; Google LLC is certified under it.

5.9 What the purchase changes

Buying "Remove Ads" ends the ads between levels and the ad banner for good; no request is made for either afterwards. The two reward videos stay optional and are then granted without a video.

6. Statistics (usage analysis)

State of this version: the analytics SDK is currently not part of the shipped package. This section applies as soon as it ships; the switch for it already exists in the game and is on by default, but it only takes effect after your consent.

6.1 What for

To see which level is too hard and which help is used. None of it serves advertising. Only game events are recorded: start of the session, a narrowly missed goal, a retry, a finished attempt (with level number and dots left), a completed level (with stars and attempts), a daily board, reward videos watched, ads between levels shown, the purchase of Remove Ads, and the consent itself.

6.2 The SDK

The statistics go through Google Analytics for Firebase. Google processes this data on my behalf (as a processor) and not as an independent controller — unlike the advertising in section 5.

6.3 What is processed

  • an app instance identifier generated by Firebase (not the advertising ID, no account, no name)
  • device and app details (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • the events named in 6.1 with their numbers

There is no name, no email address and no link to an account. Your saved game itself is not transmitted.

6.4 Legal basis, and the order

Section 25(1) TDDDG for the access to your device (the app instance identifier) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR for the processing that follows. Before your consent nothing is recorded and nothing is sent: collection in the SDK is explicitly switched off and is only released by your answer.

6.5 Withdrawal and switching off

Two ways, both effective immediately: Settings → “Send statistics” switches the statistics off, and Settings → Privacy → “Change consent” withdraws the consent (Art. 7(3) sentence 4 GDPR).

6.6 Recipient and transfer

The recipient is Google (addresses in section 3). For the transfer to the USA the same applies as in 5.8: EU-US Data Privacy Framework, adequacy decision of 10 July 2023.

9. Do you have to provide data?

No. Without consent and without a purchase the game is fully playable: all 120 levels and every daily board are reachable without money and without ads. "Again" is always instant and free.

10. Deletion

  • Uninstalling deletes all of the app's local data.
  • The advertising ID can be reset or deleted in the Android settings under "Privacy → Ads".
  • Android's Auto Backup can be removed under "Google → Backup".
  • There is no account that could be deleted — none exists.
